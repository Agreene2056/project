About Us

Know Your Horror is a fan made website inspired by fandom.com and was created for new or old horror fans who may want some information regarding their favorite horror killers and some information about the franchises. This website is built to make horror information easier to access and to understand as this website does not contain enormous amount of information unlike most websites and only a little so it's easier to read through. Some information in my website is also cited from fandom.com

What you'll find here

  • Clickable image links in "Home" page to explore character information
  • Horror movie breakdowns and summaries
  • Franchise timelines and lore explanations
  • Continuously expanding content

Heads Up

This is an early demo of the website meaning only 6 horror killers will be shown. More horror characters will be added in the future. Don't forget to check out fandom.com if you want the full information.
